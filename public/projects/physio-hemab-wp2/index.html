Recruitment monitoring

Planned measures: participants enrolled by facility and data collector, weekly recruitment, cumulative recruitment and progress against target.

Forms & Completion

Planned monitoring: Enrollment Form, Maternal Record Book, Physical Examination / Physicians Form and required form completion by participant and facility.

Activity Diary

Planned measures: expected diaries, diaries completed on the expected date, late diaries, days late, missing diaries and performance by data collector and facility.

Device tracking

Planned measures: device ID, distribution date, facility, participant, expected return date, actual return date and overdue devices. Distribution and return fields have been identified in the HeMAB Ghana Devices REDCap project (PID 411).

Data quality

Planned checks: missing required forms, late forms, incomplete records, duplicate or inconsistent records and records requiring follow-up.
